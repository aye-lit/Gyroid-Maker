The surface is cos x · sin y + cos y · sin z + cos z · sin x = 0. A patch is one sixth of the cube around a gyroid's center point, six patches make a cube, and a cell is one full repeat of the pattern. Cells tile with no gaps, so printed copies can be stacked or joined edge to edge. The weight estimate assumes 100% infill PLA.
